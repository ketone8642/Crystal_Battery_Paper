How to read a prediction

Validation precedes testing

Each candidate uses training-only preprocessing within grouped CV. Final model choices were frozen before holdout evaluation.

Transfer needs care

Na and K are absent from the training ions. Their predictions are transfer estimates without fine-tuning or calibrated uncertainty.

An adaptation of the paper

The project uses 740 composition and symmetry features. Atomic coordinates and the paper’s complete original feature definition are not included.
